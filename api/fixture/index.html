The reference guest: the smallest real harness, and what berm is measured and tested against.

Everything below the #[harness] line is what an author actually writes. The exports, the manifest section, the dispatch, and the panic handler come from the SDK.

Each tool prices or proves one thing, which is why they are not useful on their own: echo carries typed arguments across the boundary, chatty makes a hundred host calls to price one, probe allocates to show the heap arrives without a second entry into the guest, and boom fails on purpose. crates/berm/examples/measure.rs reads the numbers off them, and the tests below are the only exercise the SDK’s host-side test::call gets.
